## 1. What is Network?

Network is Singapore's interior design matching service. We hand-pick the best interior design firms for you based on your home, budget, and style, so you don't spend weeks comparing options on your own. Free to use, no obligation.

## 2. Is Network an interior design firm?

No, Network is not an interior design firm and we don't carry out renovations ourselves. We're a matching service that connects Singapore homeowners with vetted interior design firms in our network. Once you're matched, you work directly with the firms. Network is just the introduction.

## 3. How does Network work?

The process has three steps:

1. You answer a short questionnaire about your home, style, and budget.
2. Our team hand-picks 3 firms from our network of 120+ vetted designers that fit your specific needs.
3. You meet the firms, compare quotes, and choose your favourite.

It takes only a few minutes to start. Most homeowners are matched within one business day.

## 4. Is Network free for homeowners?

Yes, Network is completely free for homeowners. There is no charge to be matched, request quotes, or use any of our tools. We are paid by the interior design firm only when they win a project, never by the homeowner.

## 5. Can I trust the firms on Network?

Yes. Every firm in our network undergoes a vetting process before being listed. We verify ACRA business registration, check relevant industry licences and accreditations (HDB Registered Renovation Contractor, CaseTrust-RCMA, BCA, ISO, BizSafe where applicable), review portfolio and project history, and cross-check with industry sources.

We also monitor ongoing performance using verified reviews from homeowners who completed projects. Firms that fall below our quality standards are removed from the network.

## 6. How is Network different from other renovation platforms in Singapore?

Most renovation platforms are directories. They show you every firm and leave you to figure out which ones might be a good fit. Network is a concierge matching service. Our team studies your specific needs, then hand-picks 3 firms tailored to your home, budget, style, and timeline. You receive a curated shortlist instead of an overwhelming list.

## 7. What types of properties does Network handle?

We match for HDB flats, condominiums, and landed properties across Singapore. We handle projects ranging from minor refurbishments under $30,000 to full renovations above $150,000. Whether you are renovating a 3-room HDB BTO or a landed home, we have firms in our network that specialise in your project type.

## 8. How long does a typical Singapore renovation take?

Timelines vary by property type and scope, but as a general guide:

- **HDB flats:** 8 to 12 weeks for renovation work, plus 4 to 6 weeks for design and planning beforehand.
- **Condominiums:** Similar to HDB, sometimes longer due to MCST rules.
- **Landed properties:** 4 to 6 months or more, depending on the scope of work.

The firms you are matched with will provide specific timelines for your project.

## 9. What if I'm not happy with the firms I'm matched with?

Let us know and we will re-match you with different firms from our network, or refine the shortlist based on your feedback. There is no obligation to engage any of the firms we suggest. Our goal is to help you find the right partner, not simply any partner.

## 10. How much does HDB or condo renovation cost in Singapore?

Costs vary widely depending on property type, size, and scope. As a general guide:

- HDB BTO (light refresh): from $30,000
- HDB resale (full renovation): $50,000 to $90,000
- Condominium (mid-tier renovation): $60,000 to $120,000
- Landed property (full renovation): from $150,000

For a more accurate estimate based on your specific property and scope, please use our Cost Guide on the website.

## 11. How do you match us with designers?

Three things drive the match:

1. **Your brief** — property type (HDB, condo, landed), size, budget range, timeline, and the style you're drawn to.
2. **Firm fit** — we look at which firms in our network have done strong work on similar properties, in your budget band, and in the style direction you described.
3. **Current capacity** — we only match you with firms that can actually take on your project in your timeline, so you're not chasing a firm that's fully booked.

Our team does this manually, not with a generic algorithm. You get 3 firms shortlisted specifically for your project, usually within one business day.
